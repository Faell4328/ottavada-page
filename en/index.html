Why Ottavada?

Sheet music management is still done manually: email exchanges, pen drives, duplicate folders and sheets, and constant risk of losing the right version. Ottavada solves this.

Organization

Never waste time looking for the right sheet music again.

📂

Smart indexing

Just select a folder with sheet music. Ottavada identifies the song name from the folder and suggests instruments from the files, with no manual entry needed.

🎯

No more duplicates

The system does not allow two identical names for a piece or sheet. If there are two violins, they will be "Violin I" and "Violin II", never a duplicate "Violin".

🔍

Smart search and filters

Find any piece in seconds. Search by name or filter by category, composer, and arranger. Perfect for large collections.

🎻

Standard score order

When expanding a piece, sheet music appears in standard score order: woodwinds, brass, percussion, keyboards and strings, matching the world standard "Standard Orchestral Score Order".

⭐

Favorites and quick sections

Mark pieces as favorites and access sections like "Not allowed" or "No sheet music" with a single click. Everything at your fingertips.

🔄

Use the tools you already know

Works with Finale, MuseScore, Sibelius, Dorico and any file in PDF, MusicXML or MIDI. You keep working your way, with no changes.

Sync

One app. Two usage modes.

🎓

Conductor computer mode

Full repertoire control: add, edit and remove pieces and sheet music. Choose what goes to the rehearsal computer and what stays with you.

🎵

Rehearsal computer mode

Quick reference for the rehearsal room. Files can only be viewed, with no risk of accidentally changing or deleting anything.

☁️

Free cloud sync

Sync via Google Drive or Koofr at no extra cost. Files are compressed before upload, taking up less space and being faster.

💾

Computer broke? Nothing is lost

Formatted or switched machines? Your sheet music, categories, composers and arrangers are safe. One click and everything is back in place.

Freedom

You're not locked into the tool.

📁

Your files, your rules

Ottavada does not rename folders or files, does not move anything, and does not impose proprietary formats. Your manual organization is respected and encouraged.

🚪

Uninstalled? Your files stay

If you ever decide to stop using it, your sheet music stays exactly where it was, organized, accessible, and with no dependency on Ottavada.

🛡️

You decide what goes to rehearsal

Not everything needs to go to the rehearsal computer. You choose: this sheet can be sent, blocked, or ignored. You're in control, always.

🗄️

Local copies

Sheet music is saved on your computer. Access everything even without internet during rehearsal. Fast and no cloud dependency.

More perks

Small details that make a difference.

🌐

Multi-language

Available in Portuguese, English, Spanish, French, Italian and German, to serve musicians from anywhere.

🆓

Free, no tricks

Totally free for personal use, orchestras, bands and institutions. No subscription, no limit on pieces, and no proprietary server to maintain.

🌱

Constantly evolving

Ottavada is in early development, but receives frequent improvements. New features, fixes and languages are added based on user feedback.

☁️

No need to keep a computer on

Each computer accesses the cloud on its own. You don't need to keep the conductor computer on for the rehearsal computer to work — sync happens directly through the cloud.

💻

Open source

Ottavada is open source. You can view the code, suggest changes, and contribute on GitHub. GitHub.
