The problem for anyone who works with sheet music

Sheet music management is still done manually: message exchanges, duplicate folders, and the constant risk of playing the wrong version at rehearsal. Ottavada fixes that for good.

Who is Ottavada for?

For conductors, musicians and orchestras who work with sheet music every day. If you organize music manually, waste time searching for files or struggle with different versions between computers. Ottavada fixes this once and for all.

Organization

Find any sheet music in seconds. No more treasure hunts.

📂

Add sheet music quickly

Just select a folder with sheet music and you're done: Ottavada reads the song name from the folder and suggests the instruments from the files. No typing one by one.

🎯

No confusion: always know the right sheet

The system does not allow two identical names for a piece or sheet. If there are two violins, they will be "Violin I" and "Violin II", never a duplicate "Violin" at rehearsal.

🧹

Find duplicates you never noticed

Ottavada scans your repertoire and reveals repeated songs or sheet music that went unnoticed, even in different folders.

🔍

Find anything with incredible ease

Search by name or filter by category, composer and arranger. Perfect for orchestras with hundreds of scores.

🎻

World-standard score order

When you open a piece, sheet music appears in standard order: woodwinds, brass, percussion, keyboards and strings. The same "Standard Orchestral Score Order" used worldwide.

⭐

Favorites and shortcuts in one click

Mark pieces as favorites and quickly access sections like "Not allowed" or "No sheet music". Everything at your fingertips.

🔄

Keep using your favorite tools

Works with Finale, MuseScore, Sibelius, Dorico and any file in PDF, MusicXML or MIDI. You don't change a thing in your workflow.

Sync

One app. Two modes: conductor and rehearsal.

🎓

Conductor computer: full control

Add, edit and remove pieces and sheet music. Decide what goes to rehearsal and what stays with you. You call the shots, always.

🎵

Rehearsal computer: read-only, zero risk

Quick reference for the rehearsal room. Files can only be viewed, with no risk of someone accidentally deleting or changing a score.

☁️

Sync free via Google Drive or Koofr

No subscription fees. Files are compressed before upload, taking up less space and syncing faster.

💾

PC formatted? Back in 1 click

Switched machines or formatted the computer? Your sheet music, categories, composers and arrangers are saved. One click and everything is back in place, in the same folders as before.

Freedom

Your files, your rules. No lock-in.

📁

Your files, your rules

Ottavada does not rename folders or files, does not move anything, and does not impose proprietary formats. Your manual organization is respected and encouraged.

🚪

Uninstalled? Your sheet music stays

If one day you decide to stop using it, your sheet music stays exactly where it was, organized, accessible, and with no dependency on Ottavada.

🛡️

You decide what goes to rehearsal

Not every score needs to go to the rehearsal computer. You choose: send, block or ignore. Drafts stay only with you.

🗄️

Works offline at rehearsal

Sheet music is saved on your computer. Access everything even without internet during rehearsal. Fast, reliable, and no cloud dependency when it matters most.

More perks

Available in Portuguese, English, Spanish, French, Italian and German. For orchestras, bands and musicians worldwide.
